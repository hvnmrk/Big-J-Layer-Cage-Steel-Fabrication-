Who We Are

Engineering Excellence in Poultry Equipment

Located along Parada Road, Valenzuela City, Big J Layer Cage Steel Fabrication has built a strong reputation for delivering rugged, high-performance housing and automation tools tailored for poultry farmers.

Corrosion Resistant

Precision Automation

A high-quality, corrosion-resistant poultry cage designed for long-lasting durability.
